Our approach

Root-cause medicine, delivered as ordinary general practice.

Epica combines comprehensive GP care with integrative, functional and lifestyle medicine. That means longer consultations, careful history taking, and treatment plans built around metabolic health, hormones, chronic disease and prevention rather than symptoms alone.

Integrative

Conventional general practice alongside evidence-informed complementary care, coordinated in one place by one doctor.

Functional

Looking for what is driving your symptoms, with thorough assessment and appropriate investigation before treatment.

Lifestyle

Sustained, practical work on sleep, nutrition, movement and stress as a genuine part of your treatment.
Our team

Dr Mei Ann Choong

Specialist GP · Functional & Integrative Medicine Physician

Dr Choong is a specialist General Practitioner and Functional & Integrative Medicine Physician, with training across the UK, Europe, the USA, Asia, Australia and New Zealand. Her practice combines precision, root-cause thinking with evidence-based general practice.

She has special interests in metabolic health, hormonal balance, women's health, chronic disease management, preventative health and longevity medicine. She also serves as a Clinical Supervisor at UNSW.

Qualifications

Languages

Integrative Naturopath

Qualifications
